⚠ The Crisis

The Silent Crisis in Nigerian Agriculture

Nigerian farmers work hard to grow food — but a devastating amount is lost before it ever reaches a plate. The problem is not the farm. It is everything that happens after the harvest.

💰
₦5T
Annual Post-Harvest Losses

Nigeria loses an estimated ₦5 trillion every year to post-harvest food losses — a staggering economic and humanitarian crisis.

🍅
40%
Produce Goes to Waste

Up to 40% of all agricultural produce spoils before reaching consumers — due to poor storage, transport, and market access.

📉
30%
Farmer Income Lost

Farmers lose up to 30% of potential income to spoilage, poor pricing information, and a lack of direct buyer connections.

The challenge is not that Nigerian farmers lack skill or dedication — it is that they lack access to the right tools, information, and connections at the right time. Without AI-driven insights, real-time market data, and reliable storage networks, millions of families continue to bear the cost of a broken post-harvest system. YieldCore is designed to change that.

✨ The Solution

How YieldCore Works

Five powerful, interconnected tools — designed specifically for Nigerian farmers — working together to eliminate post-harvest losses at every stage of the supply chain.

🔬
AI Spoilage Prediction

Machine learning models analyse crop data, temperature, humidity, and transport time to predict spoilage risk — giving farmers time to act before losses occur.

🗺️
Storage Access Map

A real-time interactive map connecting farmers to nearby certified cold-storage facilities, warehouses, and preservation centres across Nigeria.

📊
Market Price Insights

Live and historical price data from markets across Nigeria, giving farmers the knowledge to sell at the right time and to the right buyers.

🤝
Farmer-to-Buyer Connection

A transparent digital marketplace connecting farmers directly to verified bulk buyers, retailers, and food processors — cutting out unnecessary middlemen and ensuring fair, transparent pricing.

📲
SMS & Email Alerts

Farmers receive timely alerts, price updates, and spoilage warnings via SMS and email — ensuring no one is left behind. All powered from your dashboard.

🚀 Potential Impact

What YieldCore Could Achieve

These are forward-looking projections based on the platform's conceptual design — representing the change we are working towards, not results already achieved.

📉
Reduce Post-Harvest Food Waste

By predicting spoilage before it occurs and connecting farmers to storage in time, YieldCore has the potential to significantly reduce the 40% of crops currently lost after harvest.

💵
Increase Farmer Income Potential

With direct buyer access and live price intelligence, farmers can negotiate better deals and retain a larger share of the value they produce.

🚚
Improve Food Distribution Efficiency

By mapping storage facilities and connecting the full supply chain — from farm to buyer to consumer — YieldCore aims to make food distribution faster, cheaper, and more reliable.

🌍
Scalable Across Nigeria and Beyond

Designed with Nigeria's diverse agricultural landscape in mind, YieldCore's modular architecture is built to scale — from Zamfara to Nigeria, and eventually across West Africa.

👥 Who It Serves

Built for the Entire Agricultural Ecosystem

YieldCore is designed to serve every stakeholder in Nigeria's food supply chain — because solving post-harvest loss requires a connected community, not a single tool.

📪
Smallholder Farmers

The primary beneficiaries — farmers who grow crops but currently lack the tools to protect their harvest and access fair markets.

👩‍🌾
Women Farmers

Women make up a significant portion of Nigeria's agricultural workforce. YieldCore provides accessible tools regardless of literacy level.

🏭
Storage Providers

Warehouses, cold-storage facilities, and silos can list their capacity and connect directly to farmers in need of preservation services.

🛒
Buyers & Retailers
